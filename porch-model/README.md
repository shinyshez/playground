# Porch model

Interactive three.js model of the porch slab, window and garden steps, with deck and mower-storage options.
Open `index.html` in a browser (needs internet for three.js from cdnjs). One finger drag to orbit, pinch to zoom, two fingers to pan.

All dimensions in cm. "Left" and "right" are as seen standing on the slab looking out to the garden.

## Existing

| Item | Dimensions |
|---|---|
| Slab | 286 long × 88 deep × 13 thick, horizontal |
| Window | Full slab width (286) × 245 tall, along the house-side long edge |
| Steps | 10 steps, each 10 rise × 30.5 going × 92 wide |
| Step position | Right side of flight is 44 from right end of slab; 150 of slab to the left of the flight |
| Total drop to garden | 100 (steps) + slab, modelled as ~110 |
| Total run of steps | 305 |
| Hedge | Runs along the left-hand side |

## Options keeping the existing steps (use the 150 strip left of the steps)

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| A | 150 × 150 deck at slab height, store beneath, double doors facing garden (~145 internal depth) |
| B | As A, store extended 60 further out, topped with a planter |
| C | 150 × 300 deck, long store beneath, doors at far end |
| D | 150 × 150 deck, then a lower flat-roofed store (roof 30 below deck, ~75 headroom), doors at far end |

Side doors were ruled out because of the hedge.

## Options with new steps

New flights: 7 risers × 15.7 rise × 28 going (~1.7 m run).

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Full-width deck 286 × 180, store beneath, flight runs along the front face on the right |
| 2 | Full-width deck 286 × 180, 110-wide central flight straight out, a store each side |

| 4 | Full-width deck 286 × 180, nested corner steps on the right-hand front corner |
| 5 | Narrow deck 286 × 120, wide store (~280 × 115 × 100 internal), 92-wide flight on the right |
| 6 | As 5, flight on the left (hedge side) |

## Rough mower size used

~60–70 wide, 130–160 long with handle folded, 100–110 tall.
