The Goblin Ha' Field Kit

We're off on a woodland walk to the ruins of Yester Castle near Gifford in East Lothian. Here's everything you need to get there and come prepared. Everything else stays a surprise.

The walk

How big an adventure?

The walk follows easy woodland tracks beside a burn. It suits most ages and has no scrambling, but the paths are very muddy in places. There and back on the same route.

Where to meet

Getting there

Short walk

Danskine Lodge

On the B6355, about 2 miles east of Gifford, opposite Danskine Loch.

Postcode EH41 4PJ · Grid ref NT 566 674

Parking is very limited. Park on a verge east of Danskine Farm and walk back to the gate opposite the loch. Don't block gates or farm tracks.

Longer walk

Gifford village

Plenty of parking opposite the village park. Follow the Yester path through the woods, about 1.5 miles each way.

Allow an extra hour or so.

There's no public transport within a mile, so sort out lifts in advance.

Timing

Beat the sunset

  • At the end of October the sun sets around 4:30–4:40pm in East Lothian.
  • Aim to set off by about 3pm so we get the golden light on the way out and are back at the cars by dusk.
  • It gets dark a little earlier under the trees, so torches come out sooner than you'd think.

Ground rules

Keep it safe, keep it lovely

The castle's underground parts have been closed to the public since 2021 for safety. We'll enjoy the ruins from outside, and nobody goes into any tunnels or vaults.
  • The ruins are a protected Scheduled Ancient Monument and aren't maintained, so no climbing on the walls.
  • Take nothing but photos. Every stone belongs to the monument.
  • Stick together in the woods, especially once the torches are on.
  • The site is free to visit, but check for any access news shortly before the day.
